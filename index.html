I. Deterministic Permanence of Supply Structure

The $GANJA token supply is strictly and permanently capped at 42,000,000 units. Minting functionality is eliminated at the smart contract level, systematically ruling out inflation risks and offering holders solid dilution protection and long-term stability.

II. Decentralized Initial Liquidity

57.14% (24,000,000 $GANJA) of total supply is initially allocated to liquidity pools on major Flare Network DEXs (BlazeSwap and SparkDEX) to ensure fair market price discovery and seamless trading execution.

III. Community Strategic Reserve & Deflationary Mechanism

The remaining 42.86% (18,000,000 $GANJA) is strategically managed for sustainable community expansion and autonomous economic loops:

  • Permanent Monthly BURN: 777,777 $GANJA sent to blackhole address monthly to reduce circulating supply.
